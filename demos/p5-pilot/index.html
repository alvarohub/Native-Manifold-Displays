Two independent p5 WEBGL sketches, two surface canvases. Drag a preview to orbit. A long stroke wraps around four cube faces; a rectangle folds over the top and bottom creases. Changing or clearing one drawing does not change the other.

What is being tested?

createCanvas(width,height,WEBGL) still creates the rectangular preview. createManifoldCanvas(surface,{frame}) creates the logical drawing support. Every contour starts from its explicit placement frame: drawing x follows the heading, y follows its perpendicular, and scale converts drawing lengths to surface lengths.

This pilot supports opaque strokes with turtle imprinting: segment lengths and turning angles are preserved by unfolding across ordinary seams. It does not solve endpoint shortest paths; a planar closed curve need not close on a curved surface. Cone-vertex hits, boundaries and stalled traces are rejected explicitly. Paths retain seam crossings rather than joining sparse endpoints through the object.

Native p5 renders this preview; no three.js is loaded. Fills, textures, text, picking, translucent-shell compositing and the full shared surface GUI are not migrated here. Existing demos are unchanged. Tested version: p5 2.3.4. See the addon API and limitations.
